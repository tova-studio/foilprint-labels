About us

Who is Foilprint Labels?

We are a long established, fabric label manufacturing firm based in Christchurch, New Zealand. Foilprint Labels Ltd is a specialist manufacturer of fabric labels. We supply printed, woven and sash labels, and we look after labelling and ribbon printing requirements.

You will find us at 82 Coleridge Street, Christchurch. The quickest way to reach us is our freephone number, 0800 365 250, or by email at info@foilprint.co.nz.
